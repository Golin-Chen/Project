# Meridian Mechanical Services Credit Analysis Case Study

A comprehensive commercial credit analysis training case for banking professionals and finance students learning credit underwriting fundamentals.

## 1. Project Overview

This repository contains a complete credit analysis framework applied to a fictional plumbing and HVAC services company seeking $800,000 in equipment financing. **Meridian Mechanical Services Ltd.** is an established contractor operating in the Hamilton and Burlington area of Ontario with 8 years of operating history and $3.35 million in 2024 revenue.

The case study demonstrates how commercial lenders evaluate credit risk across multiple dimensions including financial performance, management quality, industry dynamics, collateral coverage, and stress testing. It includes three years of financial statements, detailed ratio analysis, risk assessment, and a complete covenant package that results in an **"Approve with Exception and Conditions"** recommendation.

---

## 2. Key Objectives

After working through this case study, you will be able to:

- **Apply the 5 C's framework** (Character, Capacity, Capital, Collateral, Condition) to evaluate commercial credit applications
- **Calculate and interpret key credit ratios** including DSCR, leverage ratios, and liquidity metrics
- **Identify financial red flags** such as margin deterioration, customer concentration, and disclosure concerns
- **Design financial covenants** that protect the lender while remaining achievable for the borrower
- **Perform sensitivity and scenario analysis** to stress-test loan serviceability under adverse conditions
- **Structure conditions precedent** that address material risks before funds are advanced
- **Recommend credit decisions** with appropriate policy exceptions and risk mitigations
- **Analyze the trade-offs** between strict policy compliance and practical lending judgment

## 3. What You'll Learn

**Financial Analysis**
- Three-year income statement and balance sheet trends
- Cash flow analysis and debt service capacity testing
- Owner compensation normalization for credit purposes
- Working capital management and liquidity assessment

**Credit Risk Assessment**
- Customer concentration risk (32% of revenue from single client)
- Key person dependency on owner (Marco DeLuca)
- Margin compression and profitability deterioration
- Collateral coverage analysis and personal guarantee valuation
- Transparency and disclosure concerns

**Structural Solutions**
- How to use covenants to enforce compensation limits and dividend restrictions
- How to set minimum financial ratio thresholds that protect the lender
- How to structure conditions precedent (18 total in this case)
- How personal guarantees bridge gaps in asset-backed collateral
- Insurance requirements to mitigate key person risk

**Judgment & Decision-Making**
- When to request policy exceptions and how to justify them
- How to apply scenario analysis to test robustness of recommendations
- The importance of active monitoring throughout the facility term
- Real-world trade-offs between credit purity and commercial reality

## How to Use This Repository

**For Students:** Work through the case study independently before reviewing the complete analysis report. Calculate your own ratios, identify risks, and develop your own recommendation. Then compare your thinking to the detailed report.

**For Educators:** Use this as a capstone exercise in commercial lending, corporate finance, or credit analysis courses. Students should present their recommendations to a mock credit committee.

**For Practitioners:** Reference the covenant language, conditions precedent structure, and risk mitigation approach as templates for your own credit files. The case demonstrates how to handle policy exceptions with strong mitigating factors.
