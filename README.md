# 💰 Finance & Risk Management

### 🏦 Meridian Mechanical Services: Commercial Credit Analysis Case Study
🔗 **[View Project](https://github.com/Golin-Chen/Project/tree/main/Meridian%20Mechanical%20Services%20Credit%20Analysis)**

**What's This About?**  
A complete **credit analysis training case** showing how banks evaluate whether to approve a **$800,000 equipment loan**. Learn how lenders assess risk, design financial safeguards, and make lending decisions.

**Key Learning Points** ✨
- **Financial Analysis:** Debt service capacity, leverage ratios, profitability trends
- **Risk Assessment:** Customer concentration, collateral coverage, key person dependencies
- **Credit Decision Framework:** The "5 C's" of lending (Character, Capacity, Capital, Collateral, Condition)
- **Structural Solutions:** Financial covenants, personal guarantees, conditions for funding

**Real-World Skills:** Perfect for aspiring credit analysts, bankers, or business professionals learning how lending decisions are made.

**Tools Used:** Financial Modeling, Ratio Analysis, Risk Assessment, Scenario Testing
